{% comment %} ---------------- the summary. Every figure a property on Estimate ---- >>> ANCHOR: BOQ-LIVE-KPIS <<< ⚠⚠ THE CARDS MOVE AS THE PERCENTAGES ARE TYPED, and until now they did not. Saahil: the contingency and design fee inputs "do not move the KPI cards". They were rendered server-side and the inputs sit BELOW them, so the change was both invisible and off-screen until Save was pressed. ⚠⚠ THE ARITHMETIC STILL LIVES IN PYTHON. What the browser does here is a PREVIEW of three formulas, from a base cost the server worked out. It cannot save anything: `boq_save` recomputes every figure from the model, so the worst a wrong line of JavaScript can do is show a number that corrects itself on Save. That is the property that makes this acceptable at all — see the note on the script at the foot of this file. ⚠ THE SUB-TEXT SAYS WHAT THE CARD DOES NOT (point 9). Three of these lines used to restate the percentage typed in the box below — "5% of base" under a field containing 5 — which is worse now that the two move together. They carry the share of the QUOTE instead, which is the figure nobody had: an 8% contingency is 6.4% of what the client is billed, and that is the number that gets argued about. {% endcomment %}
